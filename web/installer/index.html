[::] LATTICE CONTROL

Join the network.

Install a system service, receive a private IP, and connect automatically through the Lattice gateway.

Administrator authorization is required. Read and accept the participation terms during setup. Joining the network does not choose a workload master: select one on your device afterward. One dashboard account can create tokens for multiple master nodes belonging to the same owner. For a master, obtain an activation token after signing in and enter it privately during setup. You can also run the master installer later on an ordinary installation; it adds the component and service while preserving your identity and private IP.

A running service becomes network ready after a fresh handshake, a verified overlay route, and a successful probe. Workloads remain subject to the device owner's policy.
